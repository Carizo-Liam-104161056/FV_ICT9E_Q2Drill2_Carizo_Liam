O.B. Montessori Center

"A Diamond Legacy of Learning"

Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, also known as OBMC is a school containing 5 campuses, Greenhills, Angeles, Las Pinas, Sta. Ana, and Fairview.



We are a private school teaching children using the Montessori method, we aim to let your child grow to become a better person for our country's future.
